# Proof Points and Metrics

## Output Trust and Interpretation
- Reduced interpretation-related support tickets after shipping the user-facing analysis interpretation layer
- Improved session completion rates in coached workflows after output interpretation redesign
- Measurable increase in user-rated feedback relevance after feedback system redesign
- Reduced "confusing feedback" flags from field operators post-calibration of feedback system

## QA and Data Quality
- Reduction in post-release output quality incidents after systematic QA process was introduced
- Improved time-to-diagnosis for model behavior issues through structured diagnostic workflows
- Reduction in release-blocking QA failures after test case library and evaluation criteria were established

## Onsite Operations
- Reduction in calibration failures and setup errors after capture workflow redesign
- Improved data validity rates from capture sessions after structured operator checklists were introduced
- Made onsite process trainable and repeatable for new operators

## Product Documentation and Alignment
- Reduction in engineering rework cycles caused by unclear requirements after PRD standards were introduced
- Faster onboarding time for new engineering and product team members
- Reduction in cross-team disagreement cycles on display criteria after interpretation standards were documented

## Prioritization and Delivery
- Shipped planned scope within the cycle without mid-cycle re-prioritization
- Stakeholder alignment maintained despite cuts, with explicit tradeoff communication

## Feedback System
- Increased feedback relevance ratings in user testing after feedback taxonomy redesign
- Reduced negative user feedback on analysis module post-interpretation layer redesign
- Feature adoption increase after rebuilding requirements around interpreted output model

## General PM Impact Language
When metrics are not available or cannot be cited precisely:
- "Meaningfully reduced"
- "Significantly improved"
- "Established a repeatable process that scaled with the team"
- "Gave stakeholders a credible visibility layer"
- "Made the system trainable and reliable for new users"

## Coaching Notes
- Always prefer specific numbers over vague language when available
- If a metric is directional but not precise, say "measurable reduction" rather than just "reduced"
- Do not fabricate specific percentages or counts that don't exist
- When asked for metrics, bridge to proof points if hard numbers are unavailable: "We didn't track it as a formal KPI, but the signal we used was..."
