# Interview Positioning Variants

## General PM

I'm a Product Manager with recent experience building AI-powered analysis products — specifically products that turn complex model outputs into trusted, understandable user experiences. I've owned product definition end-to-end in technically complex environments: from feature specs and QA standards to cross-functional alignment and user-facing design. My strength is bringing clarity and structure to ambiguous product challenges, especially where the technical and user sides of the problem need to be translated for each other.

---

## AI PM

I'm a Product Manager who has worked directly at the intersection of AI model behavior and user-facing product design. I've owned the product layer for a computer vision system — defining what outputs surface to users, how interpretation logic should work, what constitutes reliable signal, and how to build user trust in AI-generated outputs. I've also designed feedback systems that convert model outputs into contextual coaching language, including the logic for coverage, specificity, tone, and persona variation. My core strength is making AI systems usable and trustworthy, not just technically functional.

---

## Platform PM

I'm a Product Manager with experience on a technically complex platform where product, data, model behavior, and operational workflows all intersect. I've owned product definition across the full stack of user-facing concerns: interpretation layers, feedback systems, onsite workflow design, QA processes, and documentation standards. I work well at the platform level — where decisions affect many downstream experiences and alignment across technical and business teams is a constant requirement. I'm comfortable with ambiguity, strong on documentation, and focused on making complex systems work reliably and credibly for end users.

---

## B2B SaaS PM

I'm a Product Manager with experience building AI-powered products for professional users in operationally complex environments. My background includes designing user-facing analysis tools, feedback systems, and onsite capture workflows — all in a B2B context where customer trust, output credibility, and operational reliability are critical success criteria. I've worked closely with commercial teams on prioritization, managed stakeholder expectations across technical and non-technical audiences, and owned structured product documentation that gave teams and customers clear visibility into what was being built and why.

---

## Technical PM (TPM)

I'm a Product Manager with strong technical fluency and a track record of working directly with ML and engineering teams on AI system design. I've defined product requirements for computer vision outputs, designed QA evaluation frameworks for model behavior, built diagnostic workflows for output validation, and owned acceptance criteria for technically complex features. I'm comfortable reading model outputs, understanding data quality implications, and translating technical constraints into product decisions. I bridge engineering and business without pretending to be an engineer — my value is structured product thinking that keeps technical work pointed at real user and business outcomes.
