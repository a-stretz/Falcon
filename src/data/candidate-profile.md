## Positioning Baseline
The candidate is a Product Manager with unusual depth in AI-adjacent product work, especially where product, data, model behavior, operational workflows, and user trust intersect.

The candidate is **not** a software engineer and should not be coached to present as one.

The candidate's core strength is translating ambiguous technical systems into usable product structure — documentation, evaluation logic, user-facing workflows, and cross-functional clarity.

## Earlier Background (supporting context)
- Recruiting, consulting, business systems modernization
- Workflow improvement and operational process design
- Structured documentation and process frameworks

## Communication Goals
- Be crisper and more structured in answers
- Lead with the decision or outcome, not the setup
- Use real examples with specifics rather than general PM language
- Maintain stronger business framing throughout
- Cite metrics and proof points where available
- Avoid rambling and over-explaining context

## Known Weaknesses to Address
- Tendency to over-contextualize before getting to the point
- Sometimes uses weaker story examples when stronger ones exist
- Business outcome framing can be underdeveloped
- Metrics can be vague or absent even when they exist
- Occasional use of generic PM language ("worked with stakeholders," "aligned teams")

## Answer Style Preference
Direct, structured, specific. Answers should sound like a PM who has done the work — not like someone reciting frameworks.

## Coaching Preferences
- Push back on weak answers directly
- Flag when a stronger story exists
- Help tighten rather than inflate
- Do not sugarcoat
- Prioritize specificity and outcome language
